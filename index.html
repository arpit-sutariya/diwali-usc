A night of a thousand lights

BAPS is inviting USC students to celebrate Diwali at its mandir in Chino Hills. We're running a bus from campus so you can see the hand-carved mandir lit up after sunset and spend the evening with the community.

Diwali and Annakut celebrations at BAPS mandirs usually include:

  • The mandir in light. The stone shikhars and courtyard are illuminated in color once the sun goes down.
  • The Annakut. Hundreds of vegetarian sweets and dishes arranged as an offering before the murtis to welcome the Hindu New Year.
  • Rangoli and decorations. Large designs, floating diyas and festival displays throughout the grounds.
  • Performances and activities. Cultural dances, exhibits and festival activities in the halls.

Before you go

The mandir is a place of worship, so a few simple guidelines help everyone enjoy the evening.

  • Dress modestly. Shoulders and knees covered. Traditional Indian clothes are welcome but not required.
  • Shoes come off before you enter the mandir. Shoe racks are provided.
  • Keep it quiet inside. Photography isn't allowed in the main mandir hall; follow the volunteers' guidance.
  • Nothing non-vegetarian. Don't bring meat, eggs or alcohol onto the grounds.
  • Be at the pickup spot 10 minutes early. The bus leaves on time, and your QR ticket is checked as you board.
